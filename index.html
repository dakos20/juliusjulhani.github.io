Case file · Background

About Me

Who I am, where I study and where I want my career to go.

I am a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Criminology student who likes puzzles that need patience and proof. What drew me to this field is how much of justice depends on small, carefully handled details: a measurement, a note, a print on a surface.

Through my courses I have practiced crime scene documentation, fingerprint classification, report writing and court testimony. Outside class I volunteer in community safety drives, because prevention starts with people knowing their rights and the people who protect them.

I work best in a team, I write clear reports, and I stay calm when a task gets hard.
